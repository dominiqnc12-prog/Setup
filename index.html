The Yard Takeover Strategy

A high-fidelity recruitment blueprint for North Carolina campuses, designed to maximize organizational visibility through a low-friction social funnel.

The Strategy Pillars

  • 01. High-Production Hook

    Utilizing professional lighting and top-tier NC DJs to create a "can't miss" social atmosphere.

  • 02. Recruitment Integration

    Intermission segments dedicated to introducing the org's mission without breaking the event's energy.

  • 03. Digital Funnel

    QR codes placed at the entrance and on screens to capture attendee info for the follow-up meeting.

Event Show Flow

9:00 PM — DOORS & ENERGY

DJ set begins at low volume. Light show active. Photo booth opens.

10:30 PM — THE MISSION

Music cuts. President's welcome. 5-minute video presentation on the Yard screens.

11:00 PM — PEAK VIBE

Main headliner set. QR codes for Interest Meeting distributed via giveaway cards.

Risk Mitigation Matrix

Security Violation

Strict ID check at door. Bag policy enforced by hired off-duty RPD/Campus PD.

Recruitment Bottleneck

If "Interest Meeting" signups are < 20 by 11 PM, deploy street team with incentive tablets.

Technical Failure

Backup generator and secondary sound console pre-staged in Green Room.
